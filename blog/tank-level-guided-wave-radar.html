Tank level and overfill with one radar sensor

The level in millimetres and a hard “full” switch from one sensor in the tank lid.

11 October 2026 · Andrii Shylenko

The problem

A metal tank with a water-based medium, such as a washer rinse tank or a coolant reservoir, is refilled by the PLC. Two float switches give full and empty but nothing in between, and a stuck float overfills the tank. The operator only learns the tank is low when the alarm comes.

The station

  • Sensor ifm LR2050, guided wave radar, G 3/4, probe 150 to 2000 mm (ordered separately), port class A
  • Master SICK SIG200, 4 ports, EtherNet/IP. 500 mA per port, 3 A total
  • Port X1, 4.6 m route, 10 m cable

The settings that matter

SettingValueWhyDatasheet / IODD
Set point SP800 mmFull: OUT1 switches on hereSP 15 to L−30 mm, steps of 1 mm
Reset point rP780 mmOUT1 drops after 20 mm of drainingrP 10 to L−35 mm, hysteresis over 5 mm
Probe length L1000 mm (our choice)Limits SP to 970 mm and rP to 965 mmInactive zone 30 mm at the top, 10 mm at the bottom
Low alarm150 mm, in the PLCRead from the level value, above the bottom inactive zoneMeasuring error ±7 mm, resolution 1 mm

The studio does not know your probe length yet. It checks against the longest probe, 2000 mm, so check L−30 yourself. It does check that rP sits below SP. The EMC figures in the datasheet assume a closed metal tank; a plastic or open tank falls under a different EMC class.

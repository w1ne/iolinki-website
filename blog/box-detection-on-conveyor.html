Box detection on a conveyor

Count the boxes, ignore the guard rail behind them, and get the distance to every box over IO-Link.

11 October 2026 · Andrii Shylenko

The problem

A photoelectric sensor across a belt counts boxes, but it also counts the guard rail on the far side, a reflective label, or a person walking past. Miscounts stop the next station or ship a short pallet.

The station

  • Sensor ifm O5D100, laser distance with background suppression, class 1 laser, port class A
  • Master Turck TBEN-S2-4IOL, 4 ports, PROFINET, EtherNet/IP or Modbus TCP
  • Port X1, 4.7 m route, 10 m cable. The C0 to C3 supply is not short-circuit proof (4 A per group), so protect V2 in the cabinet

The settings that matter

SettingValueWhyDatasheet / IODD
Switching distance500 mm (raw 50 cm)Everything closer is a box; the rail beyond is ignoredMeasuring range 0.03 to 2 m on 90 % white; IODD SP1 5 to 200 cm
Outputnormally open24 V while a box is in frontTwo complementary outputs
Box gapcheck on siteGap ÷ belt speed must be longer than one switching cycleSwitching frequency 15 Hz

The 2 m range is for white paper at 90 % remission. Brown cardboard reflects less, so check the switch point with your real boxes. Extraneous light on the object must stay under 8 klx.

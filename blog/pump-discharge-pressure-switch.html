Pump discharge pressure that does not chatter

One clean “pressure OK” signal for the PLC, with a set point and a reset point so the output does not flicker on pump ripple.

11 October 2026 · Andrii Shylenko

The problem

A pump with a running motor is not a pump that delivers. A dry run, a closed valve or a broken coupling only shows up as missing discharge pressure. A single threshold near the working pressure makes the output flicker with every pressure pulse, and the PLC logs a fault each time.

The station

  • Sensor ifm PN7092, 0 to 100 bar, liquids, G 1/4, port class A
  • Master Siemens SIMATIC ET 200eco PN 4 IO-Link (6ES7148-6JA00-0AB0)
  • Port X1, 5.7 m route, 10 m cable. Siemens allows 20 m unshielded for IO-Link

The settings that matter

SettingValueWhyDatasheet / IODD
Set point SP140 bar“Pressure OK” from here up. Our pump runs a little under 50 bar; use your pump curveSP 1 to 100 bar, steps of 0.5 bar
Reset point rP137 barDrops only below this. The 3 bar gap must be larger than the ripplerP 0.5 to 99.5 bar, at least 0.5 bar below SP
Outputnormally open24 V while pressure is OKNO/NC parameterisable

The studio checks the ranges and that rP is below SP; we checked the 0.5 bar minimum gap by hand. 120 bar is refused: the PN7092 stops at 100. Switch-on and switch-off delays (0 to 50 s) are left at the factory value; the studio does not set them yet.

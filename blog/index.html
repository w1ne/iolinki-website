IO-Link applications from real plants, built step by step in the station studio. Every post links to the station it describes.

  • A stainless part at a conveyor stop, detected with an IO-Link inductive sensor

    The station only clamps or picks when the part is really sitting on the stop. Here the parts are stainless, and that changes the setting.

    11 October 2026 · Open the station

  • Pump discharge pressure: a switch point, a reset point and nothing that chatters

    The PLC needs one clean signal: the pump has built pressure. With a set point and a reset point the signal does not chatter when the pressure ripples around the limit.

    11 October 2026 · Open the station

  • Tank level and overfill with one guided wave radar sensor

    One sensor in the tank lid gives the PLC the level in millimetres and a hard “full” switch that does not depend on the PLC program.

    11 October 2026 · Open the station

  • Coolant flow on a machine tool: teach the normal flow, switch on a percentage

    If the coolant stops, the tool and the part pay for it within seconds. A flow sensor in the coolant line stops the cycle before that happens and reports the coolant temperature too.

    11 October 2026 · Open the station

  • Box detection on a conveyor with a laser distance sensor

    A distance sensor across the belt sees the boxes and ignores the guard rail behind them. Over IO-Link it also tells you how far away each box was.

## Product-family scope

A product family is the named commercial product range recorded in the agreed
quote, including variants, successors and hardware revisions within that range.
Manufactured-unit counts, customer counts and variants within the family are
unlimited and royalty-free. An unrelated product line needs a separate license.
Changing MCU or PHY within the agreed family does not invalidate the license or
require a new license; porting and hardware validation are separately scoped and
quoted. Enterprise quotes cover multiple families, subsidiaries or broader SDK
redistribution as explicitly agreed.

## Package and assistance scope

Both packages receive the same source, documentation, reference release and
software rights. Engineering hours cover the tasks and deliverables agreed in
the quote. Agree the target board, compiler, PHY, tasks and booking window before
scheduling. Larger ports and additional validation work are quoted separately.
Reproducible stack bug corrections do not consume custom engineering hours.
Build and validation evidence is published with the references.

Both packages are held by the named legal company or a sole trader acting for
their own business, with a technical contact recorded in the quote. Authorized
employees and contractors working on the licensed family may use and modify the
source without developer-seat limits. Contractors receive access for that
licensee's project; they do not receive independent reuse or redistribution
rights. Subsidiaries and other separate legal entities require agreed additional
scope. Transfer rights, upgrade credit and additional renewal coverage are
recorded in the quote.

## When do I need this commercial license?

iolinki is dual-licensed. You may always use it **free of charge under the GPLv3**
(see the LICENSE file) — including in production — **provided you comply with the
GPLv3**, i.e. any work you distribute that incorporates iolinki is also licensed
under the GPLv3 (source available).

A **commercial license is required only if you distribute a closed-source or
otherwise proprietary product** that incorporates iolinki and therefore cannot
meet the GPLv3 copyleft obligations. It grants the same code under proprietary
terms, with no copyleft requirement.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What happens after the 1-year update period?**
A: You retain perpetual rights to use the licensed version and continue shipping the agreed product family. Optional annual updates/support renewal is listed at 20% of the original license fee; coverage is recorded in the quote.

**Q: Can I use iolinki in open-source projects?**
A: Yes — free of charge under the GPLv3, including in production, as long as your
project complies with the GPLv3 (source available under a GPLv3-compatible license).
A commercial license is only needed for closed-source / proprietary products.

**Q: Do you offer volume discounts?**
A: Yes, for Enterprise arrangements or multiple product families. Contact us for details.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: SEPA bank transfer, wire transfer, and invoicing (Net 30 for established companies).

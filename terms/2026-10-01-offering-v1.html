## Package and assistance scope

Both tiers receive the same usable source, documentation and reference release.
Reference build evidence and remaining validation limits are published separately;
included assistance does not promise a completed port, supplied hardware, physical
validation or conformity certification. Reproducible product bug corrections do
not consume the custom porting assistance allowance.

Single is held by the named individual, not by an employer or other legal entity.
Employer-project use, transfer rights, upgrade credit, assistance booking windows
and any additional renewal coverage must be agreed in the quote. Team is held by
the named legal company; subsidiary and other separate-entity rights require a
separate quote.

## When do I need this commercial license?

iolinki is dual-licensed. You may always use it **free of charge under the GPLv3**
(see the LICENSE file) — including in production — **provided you comply with the
GPLv3**, i.e. any work you distribute that incorporates iolinki is also licensed
under the GPLv3 (source available).

A **commercial license is required only if you distribute a closed-source or
otherwise proprietary product** that incorporates iolinki and therefore cannot
meet the GPLv3 copyleft obligations. It grants the same code under proprietary
terms, with no copyleft requirement.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What happens after the 1-year update period?**
A: You retain perpetual rights to use the version you licensed. Extended support contracts available at 20% of original license fee per year.

**Q: Can I use iolinki in open-source projects?**
A: Yes — free of charge under the GPLv3, including in production, as long as your
project complies with the GPLv3 (source available under a GPLv3-compatible license).
A commercial license is only needed for closed-source / proprietary products.

**Q: Do you offer volume discounts?**
A: Yes, for Enterprise licenses or multiple Team licenses. Contact us for details.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: SEPA bank transfer, wire transfer, and invoicing (Net 30 for established companies).
